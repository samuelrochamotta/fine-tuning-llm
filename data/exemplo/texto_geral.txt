When you learn to ride a bicycle, the hardest part is not pedaling but balance. A moving bicycle is surprisingly stable, because small turns of the handlebars keep the wheels under the rider's center of mass. Most people learn this without ever thinking about it, which is why the skill is so hard to explain and so easy to keep once you have it.

Sleep is not a passive state. During the night the brain cycles through several stages, each with its own pattern of electrical activity. Some stages seem to help consolidate memories from the day, while others are linked to physical recovery. People who sleep too little for many nights in a row make more mistakes and find it harder to concentrate.
